C. Diphtheria
D. Measles

**Ответ:** ### Рассуждение
Ans. A. Kawasaki diseaseKawasaki disease is an acute febrile illness with vasculitis having predilection for the coronary arteries. It is characterized by fever, conjunctival injection, erythema of palms and soles, polymorphous exanthem, cervical lymphadenopathy and mucocutaneous lesions.
