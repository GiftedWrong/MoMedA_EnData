### Сэмпл 1
**Вопрос:** All are true about Rinne's test except ?
A. Positive in normal ear
B. Positive in sensorineural hearing loss
C. Minimum 15-20 dB air bone gap is required in conductive deafness
D. Bone conduction is better in sensorineural hearing loss

**Ответ:** ### Рассуждение
Rinne's test: It is used to differentiate between conductive and sensorineural hearing loss. In this test, the patient is asked to hear a tone of 256 Hz through air conduction and bone conduction. If the bone conduction is better than air conduction, it is called positive Rinne's test. This indicates that there is a conductive hearing loss. If the air conduction is better than bone conduction, it is called negative Rinne's test. This indicates that there is a sensorineural hearing loss.

### Предварительный диагноз
Answer: B. Positive in sensorineural hearing loss

### Сэмпл 8
**Вопрос:** All are ototoxic drugs except -
A. Streptomycin
B. Vancomycin
C. Furosamide
D. Atropine

**Ответ:** ### Рассуждение
Ans. is 'd' i.e., Atropine Ototoxic drugs Ototoxic drugs are those which cause damage to the inner ear or auditory nerve. They are divided into two categories: 1) Drugs that are ototoxic by virtue of their chemical structure, e.g., aminoglycosides (streptomycin, gentamycin, neomycin), furosemide, cisplatin, vancomycin, etc. 2) Drugs that are ototoxic as a result of their pharmacological action, e.g., salicylates, aspirin, phenytoin, carbamazepine, etc.

### Предварительный диагноз
Answer: D. Atropine
